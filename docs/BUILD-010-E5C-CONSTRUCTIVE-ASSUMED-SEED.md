# Build 010-E5C — Constructed Conditional Seed

A prior, complete, two-semicircle cutter-center path of radius `r` traversed by a cutter of radius `R` covers the disk of radius `R + r` around the common center **if `r < R`**. At radial distance `d`, the nearest point on that full centerline circle is `|d-r|` away; every point with `0 ≤ d ≤ R+r` is therefore covered by a cutter disk in exact geometry. E5C checks the native two-arc geometry and subtracts a 0.000001 mm margin from the derived radius to account for its accepted point tolerances. It demands an interior margin and carries the claimed prior depth. It rejects incomplete loops, disconnected or nonconcentric arcs, an annular hole (`r ≥ R`), invalid dimensions and depth.

The acceptance passes the derived disk into E5B and verifies that a later, smaller cutter can move inside it at a supported depth, while out-of-disk and too-deep paths fail. The assumed prior sweep is **not authenticated**: this module does not know whether it was executed, whether its own entry was safe, or whether it reached the claimed depth. Thus the construction must not be used as stock-history authority or recursively justify its own entry. Radial engagement of later cuts remains unproven. No production operation, UI, preflight, persistence or NC dispatch is enabled.
