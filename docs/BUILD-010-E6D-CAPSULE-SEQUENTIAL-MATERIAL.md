# Build 010-E6D — Ordered Native Capsule Material Assessment

E6D extends the private E6B/E6C ordered assessment to a native capsule consisting of two straight lines and two tangent semicircles. It remains a contour reference and introduces no pocket clearing or general arbitrary-contour support.

The new capsule binding proves both source and guide are native capsules, with the same spine centers, primitive order and winding, concentric arc attachments, and a radius change equal to the signed cutter-center offset. Finite offset metadata, side, contour identity and segment count must agree. The previous E3 candidate proof remains unchanged; the new bound-source API is required by E6D before generation and again for the complete candidate. Thus a geometrically plausible guide with a different source or forged offset metadata cannot claim protected-contour binding.

E2 derives the free side and effective uniform radius, including conservative reduction on tight inside caps. The existing semantic generator retains native LINE/ARC geometry. Incoming links and complete loops are assessed against the previous assumed disk before any removal is credited, including cycles crossing LINE/ARC transitions. Closure is assessed separately against the final disk and earns no extra clearance. Any failure discards the whole candidate.

Acceptance covers inside/outside, both contour and loop windings, translated and rotated fixtures, radius reduction, complete native geometry parity, independent circumference and propagated-disk coverage samples across the whole chain and closure, depth propagation, input immutability, frozen-seed rejection, large progress and invalid seed/policy/data. It rejects circles, displaced source capsules, sign-forged offsets and geometrically inconsistent offsets even when their reported validation metadata matches. `check:010e6d` is registered in CI.

Release boundary: native capsules, one constant target depth, assumed initial clearance and successful prior planned material removal. The boundary is a cutter-center guide proof; full seed/ramp/cutter-envelope part safety is not established. Circumference exposure is not feed-relative radial engagement, chip thickness or cutting force. General closed contours, corners, ramp entry, depth orchestration, UI, persistence, operation dispatch, preflight and NC release remain closed. Fixture values are not cutting recommendations.
